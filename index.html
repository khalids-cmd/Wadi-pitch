The idea

Coffee after the day ends.

WADI is a contemporary London coffee house built for a different rhythm — specialty coffee, exceptional dessert and a carefully designed place to meet, from afternoon into the evening. Inspired by the social café culture of the Gulf. Designed for London.

What WADI is
01

Coffee

Espresso · Filter · V60 · Cold brew · Coffee of the day

Serious coffee without the ceremony. A considered programme of espresso and rotating single-origin coffees, brewed simply and consistently.

02

Dessert

A small menu worth remembering. WADI favours a deliberately limited collection of exceptional desserts rather than an oversized display. Texture, flavour and presentation matter equally.

03

Late

Coffee shouldn't disappear when the evening begins. WADI is being designed to remain relevant later than the traditional London specialty café — somewhere to meet, talk and continue the evening.

The space

Designed to stay.
Not to settle in.

WADI balances comfort with movement. Window seating, compact tables and carefully planned social spaces create somewhere comfortable enough to enjoy, without becoming a coworking lounge. The environment changes naturally from afternoon into evening through light, music and product.

Design language

Everything has a place.

Visual simplicity comes from operational discipline. WADI is designed around concealed storage, deliberate materials and a working bar that remains clean even during service.

Coffee programme

From espresso to filter.

The core menu begins with espresso and milk-based coffee, supported by a rotating filter programme. Coffee of the Day offers an accessible everyday brew. V60 introduces carefully selected single-origin coffees served hot or iced. Cold brew extends the same approach into the evening.

Bright, concentrated and intense — the base for every milk drink on the menu.

Body
Dessert

Dessert is not an afterthought.

WADI's dessert menu will be deliberately small. Each product must earn its place through flavour, texture and presentation.

The objective is simple: something worth returning for.

Influence

Inspired by a different coffee culture.

Across cities such as Riyadh and Jeddah, coffee houses form part of the evening's social rhythm. WADI takes inspiration from that culture — not by recreating it, but by translating its sense of hospitality, design and late-evening relevance into London.

Location

Marble Arch / Edgware Road

WADI is currently searching for its first home around Marble Arch and Edgware Road — a district where London's daytime economy meets an established evening hospitality culture.
