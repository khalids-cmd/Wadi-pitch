A coffee house that doesn't close at six.

WADI is a specialty coffee and dessert house in central London, open from early afternoon into the late hours. Influenced by the unhurried, late-opening café culture of the Gulf — a calm room to sit in, alone or with friends, for longer than London usually allows.

The gap

The hours nobody is building for

Most specialty coffee shops in London pull their shutters by six. What's left from nine until close is built almost entirely around drinking. If you want to sit somewhere calm at ten — with a friend, or on your own — London gives you a booth in a chain restaurant, or your own sofa. WADI simply stays open.

The concept

Four things WADI is, at once

One room, open long hours, held together by four deliberate choices rather than a long menu.

Coffee-led

Espresso, batch brew, V60, cold brew — credible quality without an oversized menu.

Dessert-driven

A compact cabinet of exceptional, bespoke desserts designed for attachment and destination appeal.

Alcohol-free, by nature

A calm evening environment for friends, couples, young professionals, Muslims and Gulf visitors — not a bar with the alcohol removed.

Design-conscious

Warm brown, cream and stone, restrained lighting, and the operational side of the business kept out of sight.

One brand, several channels

The brand stays universal — وادي / WADI, contemporary coffee, no need to explain anyone's background to walk in. Marketing is where the segmentation happens: acquisition leans on TikTok and Instagram for younger London customers, and adds Snapchat, WhatsApp and an Arabic Google/Maps presence for Gulf visitors already in the city, where those channels see substantially higher engagement.
